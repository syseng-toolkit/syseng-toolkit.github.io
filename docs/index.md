# syseng-toolkit

Free systems engineering templates for small teams, in spreadsheets that work in Google Sheets and Excel.

[Get started](get-started.md){ .md-button .md-button--primary } [See the templates](templates/index.md){ .md-button }

## Why we made it

Systems engineering keeps a project's needs, constraints and problems clear to everyone, so you build what you meant to build. The tools for it are usually expensive, proprietary, or made for large programs, like [SysML](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Systems_modeling_language) modeling tools.

This toolkit is a free baseline: a standard set of documents with professional traceability, based on [ISO/IEC/IEEE 15288](https://sebokwiki.org/wiki/ISO/IEC/IEEE_15288), that a small team can pick up in an afternoon and adapt to how it works. The tools are also meant to be used for education about systems engineering practices.

## Design philosophy

- **For small teams** of 3 to 10, and "hobbyists who want to do it a little better".
- **Learn it in five minutes.** 
- **No proprietary software.** 
- **Free and open.** 
- **Based on ISO/IEC/IEE 15288.**
