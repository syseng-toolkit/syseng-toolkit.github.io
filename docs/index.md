## Why we made it

Systems engineering keeps a project's needs, constraints and problems clear to everyone, so you build what you meant to build. The tools for it are usually expensive, proprietary, or made for large programs, like [SysML](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Systems_modeling_language) modeling tools.

This toolkit is a free baseline: a standard set of documents with professional traceability, based on [ISO/IEC/IEEE 15288](https://sebokwiki.org/wiki/ISO/IEC/IEEE_15288), that a small team can pick up in an afternoon and adapt to how it works. Before the creation of this project, we could not find quality, open-source systems engineering templates, and we hope to serve that need.

Beyond the value the templates may add to engineering projects, the exercise of templating engineering processes to be broadly applicable has been a valuable learning experience for us. We hope that these templates similarly provide value and educational opportunities to the broader community. Thank you!

## Design philosophy

- **For small teams** and hobbyists who want to do it a little better.
- **Learnable in five minutes.**
- **No proprietary software.**
- **Free and open.**
- **Based on ISO/IEC/IEEE 15288.**

## The templates

The templates follow [ISO/IEC/IEEE 15288](https://sebokwiki.org/wiki/ISO/IEC/IEEE_15288), arranged as a V-model: define down the left, build at the bottom, verify up the right. [See all templates →](templates/index.md)
