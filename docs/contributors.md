## Suggesting a change

Found a bug, or have an idea for a template? Open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/syseng-toolkit/syseng-toolkit.github.io/issues). Say which template and version (the last row of its `Template History` tab) and what you expected.

The templates are free under [CC BY 4.0](license.md).
